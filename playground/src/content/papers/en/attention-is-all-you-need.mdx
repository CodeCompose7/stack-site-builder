## Why it matters \{#why}

Replaced recurrence with self-attention, enabling parallel training and long-range
dependencies — the architecture behind every modern LLM.

## Key ideas \{#key-ideas}

- Scaled dot-product attention and multi-head attention
- Positional encodings instead of recurrence
- Encoder–decoder stacks built purely from attention and feed-forward layers

## The attention formula \{#formula}

Inline math sits in a double-dollar span: $$\mathrm{softmax}(z)_i = e^{z_i} / \sum_j e^{z_j}$$.
A fence on its own lines renders a centred display block:

$$
\mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V
$$

Three dollars work the same, which keeps a fence readable when the body itself
contains `$$`:

$$$
\mathrm{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{Concat}(\mathrm{head}_1, \dots, \mathrm{head}_h) W^O
\quad\text{where}\quad
\mathrm{head}_i = \mathrm{Attention}(Q W_i^Q, K W_i^K, V W_i^V)
$$$

Prices are left alone: the encoder–decoder base model trained for $12 hours on
8 GPUs, and the big model for $84 hours.
